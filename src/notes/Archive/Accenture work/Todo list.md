# AWS card application
Dear colleague,

as a newjoiner you have been requested to have an AMEX card by your lead. I would like to ask you to fill in the application and deliver it to us. Please, read the current [AMEX card policy](https://ts.accenture.com/sites/Accenture%20Bratislava%20Information%20Portal/TimeExpense%20Travel%20and%20Taxi/SK_American%20Express%20Card.pdf) and as a next step, please, fill out the application through [Apply for AMEX card](https://ts.accenture.com/sites/Slovakia_FS_Portal/external/Business%20travel/Forms/AllItems.aspx?RootFolder=%2Fsites%2FSlovakia%5FFS%5FPortal%2Fexternal%2FBusiness%20travel%2FCorporate%20individual%20AMEX%20card%2FApplication%20forms&InitialTabId=Ribbon%2EDocument&VisibilityContext=WSSTabPersistence).

Information to the filling out the application:

v  Please, fill out **ONLY** part 1 (Application Details) and 2 (Individual Declaration)!

v  **Deliver the application** (only page 1 and 2) to the reception for travel team or send me the scans / photos of the documents by mail

v  To the application, please, enclose the scans of **the documents below, we need 4 documents together:**

**Acceptable documents for proof of likeness** (we need 2 documents with pictures)

- National ID
- passport
- driving license

**Acceptable combinations for proof of address** (we need 2 documents with address)

- Back of national ID
- Bank statement (you can cover the amounts) – document cannot be older than 3 months
- Utility bill – gas, water, electric (you can cover the amounts) – document cannot be older than 3 months
- Home internet invoice (you can cover the amounts) – document cannot be older than 3 months
- **Phone bill is NOT valid**

o    **Do not edit** the scanned documents in editing apps (do not crop the images!)

- In case you use the account statement as the proof of the address, **you can cover the sums**
- **please keep the Office address box ticked in the Card Delivery part on the first page**

v  AMEX card is usually issued and delivered within **4-6 weeks**

If you need to pay for the company expenses sooner, you can ask for the cashless advance below:

[how to request cashless advance](https://ts.accenture.com/sites/Accenture%20Bratislava%20Information%20Portal/Finance/te/pages/Cashless%20Advance.aspx)
