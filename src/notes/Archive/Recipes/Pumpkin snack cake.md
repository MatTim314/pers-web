### Ingredients

- 1 ½ cup 375g [sugar](https://amzn.to/3rBophX)
- 2 cups 500g [all-purpose flour](https://amzn.to/37jgqNf)
- 2 teaspoons [baking powder](https://amzn.to/3xbTqtM)
- 1 teaspoon [baking soda](https://amzn.to/3i6n6Ek)
- 1 teaspoon [cinnamon](https://amzn.to/3f4Ip7r)
- ½ teaspoon [nutmeg](https://amzn.to/3xeO94Y)
- ½ teaspoon [ground ginger](https://amzn.to/3y8hJtT)
- ¼ teaspoon [ground cloves](https://amzn.to/3xd3a6Z)
- ½ teaspoon [salt](https://amzn.to/3l257Rr)
- 4 [eggs](https://www.target.com/p/vital-farms-pasture-raised-grade-a-large-eggs-12ct/-/A-18783617?aflt=plt#lnk=sametab) lightly beaten
- 1 can (15 ounces) pumpkin puree
- 1 cup 237ml [vegetable oil](https://www.target.com/p/vegetable-oil-48oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-78376311)
- ½ cup [sour cream](https://www.target.com/p/daisy-pure-38-natural-sour-cream-16oz/-/A-13451688?aflt=plt#lnk=sametab)
- ~~1 cup toffee pieces~~ Some lotus biscuits

#### Cinnamon Sugar Topping:

- 2 tablespoons [sugar](https://amzn.to/3rBophX)
- 1 teaspoon [cinnamon](https://amzn.to/3f4Ip7r)

#### Cream Cheese Drizzle:

- 2 ounces [cream cheese](https://www.target.com/p/philadelphia-original-cream-cheese-8oz/-/A-12958523) softened
- 1 tablespoon [unsalted butter](https://www.target.com/p/land-o-lakes-unsalted-butter-1lb/-/A-13227053?aflt=plt) softened
- ¾ cup [powdered sugar](https://amzn.to/2VigrOn)
- 1 teaspoon [vanilla extract](https://amzn.to/2TCE9EG)
- 2 tablespoons [milk](https://www.target.com/p/2-reduced-fat-milk-1gal-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-13276204?aflt=plt#lnk=sametab)
- pinch of kosher salt

### Instructions

- Begin by preheating your oven to 350 degrees F. Spray a 9×13 baking pan with baking spray. Set aside.
    

- In a large bowl whisk together the sugar, flour, flaxseed, baking powder, baking soda, spices, and salt. Add the eggs, pumpkin puree, vegetable oil, and sour cream. Mix to combine.
    
- Add in the toffee pieces. Gently mix to combine.
    
- Spread the batter into your prepared baking pan.
    
- In a small bowl, prepare the topping by whisking together the sugar and cinnamon. Sprinkle evenly on top of the cake.
    
- Bake for 30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean. Allow the cake to cool in the pan.
    
- Prepare the cream cheese drizzle by beating together the cream cheese, butter, powdered sugar, vanilla extract, milk, and salt.
    
- Drizzle over the cooled cake.
