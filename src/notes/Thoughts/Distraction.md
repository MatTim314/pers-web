We lead unfathomably distracted lives. Deceive the eyes, entire mind is deceived. How can you notice you're distracted when you're distracted. It's a flawless design. A loop, a dimension of existence, where your next problem is implanted into your head. And you start to believe in its gospel, you become agitated when there is no next problem, and as soon as this phantom notices your craving he delivers you onto your next outrage, your next voyage for social justice, your next reason to fume foam at the corners of your mouth. Your life becomes smaller, becomes a thing not to be concerned with, since there are larger monsters on the horizon. So you keep staring into the distance, you keep shouting at the sun-eating shadows behind the water, your fist raised and your voice loud. You keep yourself invested in the war which couldn't care less about your raised fist. The more you engage the more your own life trickles away, becoming even smaller than you already perceived it to be, becoming even less relevant than the wars out there, in the Great Distance. What you had to be convinced was important, now becomes that which your own mouth utters to others, convincee becomes the convincer. And your life keeps trickling away, noticed at best by close friends, maybe a snarky comment from a family member at a reunion once a year. But those comments, like old, crusty, in faded out color life rafts, eventually disappear, people give up on trying to convince you it's not important when they see it only further fuels your outrage. And your life keeps trickling away.
