I try to be better but often i find myself going backwards. I promised myself not to cry. I was tired of crying. I cried often, usually unprovoked. It had been a standard response to the demands of my surroundings. Things which made me sad, also made me cry. No negative emotion came without tears. And so I'd stopped. Told myself to cut it out, avoided difficult situations and distanced myself every time it was about to happen. It was a success. Outcries reduced in numbers as did my general involvement in events. The rule was simple.

> *The more invested you are in something, the more likely you are to cry*

How does one avoid crying then? Do everything with half the effort, never become passionate about anything and never allow anyone to say even half a word about anything you've been working on. It might be a **bad** word you know. No way you'd let yourself hear it and take it to heart dear god. Simple solution to an easy problem.
