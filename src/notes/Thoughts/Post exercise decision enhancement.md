[[Workout planning]]
Post workout I often experience this feeling of ease of making decisions, no matter how hard or annoying they might be. It's the feeling of gaining freedom by doing. That thinking about your decisions is the prison and taking action is the freedom. And after I exercise I don't spend any extra time on deciding the unimportant. 

Example:

> I take an apple into my hand.
> *I should wash it*.
> I go and wash it.
> *I want it split and cored*.
> I grab a knife and split the apple in half, taking out the core as well. 
> *Shouldn't I wash the knife and put it back into the holder?* 
> Doesn't matter, I'm the only one here so no one will be inconvenienced and I'll be eating another apple in a few hours anyway.

These small decisions usually feel heavy. But not after exercise. Then, they feel as light as I do. Featherweight.
