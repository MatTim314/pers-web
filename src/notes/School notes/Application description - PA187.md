IntelliBrew - Coffee maker intelligent system

**Application Description:**

IntelliBrew is an integrated application designed to enhance the functionality and user experience of smart coffee makers. At its core, the application serves as a bridge between the user and the coffee machine, offering customization, scheduling, inventory management, and maintenance tracking. The application is built around five primary data entities, which are critical to its operation:

1. **User Profiles:** The application allows multiple users to create individual profiles. Each profile captures the user's preferred settings, such as coffee strength, cup size, and preferred coffee type (e.g., espresso, latte, cappuccino).
    
2. **Coffee Preferences:** Users can create and save custom coffee profiles. This includes the ability to adjust variables like water temperature, brew time, and grind size to create the perfect cup of coffee tailored to their taste.
    
3. **Machine Settings:** The app provides a user-friendly interface to control the coffee maker’s settings. It can schedule brewing times, adjust the coffee maker's operational status, and receive notifications when the coffee is ready.
    
4. **Supply Management:** Through the app, users can track the levels of coffee beans, milk, and water. It notifies when supplies are low and can even integrate with online retailers to order supplies directly from the app.
    
5. **Maintenance Records:** The app keeps a log of cleaning and descaling cycles, filter changes, and other maintenance activities. It reminds users when it’s time for maintenance and provides step-by-step guides.
    

**Visual Components:**

- **Input Images:**
    
    1. Home screen with user profile selection.

    3. Scheduling interface for setting up future brews.
    4. Supply management screen with levels of beans and water.
    5. Maintenance screen with maintenance history and alerts.
- **Output Images:**
    
    1. Notification image when the coffee is ready.
    2. Alert image for low supplies with a direct link to order more.
    3. Coffee brewing in progress with a countdown timer.
    4. Completed maintenance task confirmation with date and time log.
    5. Summary of the user's coffee consumption statistics over time.

**Functionality:**

- **Personalization:** Each user can save multiple coffee brewing profiles, adapting to the time of day or specific coffee desires.
    
- **Smart Scheduling:** The app allows users to schedule their coffee times, accommodating to their daily routines.
    
- **Maintenance Ease:** Automated maintenance reminders with tutorials ensure the longevity and cleanliness of the coffee maker.
    
- **Supply Management:** Integrated supply tracking with one-click purchasing makes running out of coffee or filters a problem of the past.
    
- **Community:** Users can share their custom coffee profiles with the community, rate them, and try profiles created by others.
    

IntelliBrew transforms your coffee maker into a smart, attentive barista, dedicated to crafting the perfect cup every time.
