## Hateful writer
I wanted a web app where I could write and every time i press spacebar the word would dissapear as to not bother me anymore, to not weigh me down. It was supposed to be a good way to get my thoughts out without obsessing over how well written the result is. 
- I've had progress on the concept since then. I wanted to create a writing assistant, basically the same thing as when you're writing on your phone and there's the bar with suggested words so you just click the word and you don't have to worry about typos and spelling mistakes. So **what if** pressing a button it could show you a list of synonyms of the words and a different buttons for antonyms? Real time? That is you would be writing the word 'exciting' but will feel like that's not quite it. So you press SHIFT+S and a list of synonyms pops up, half above, half below your word. Then you just press down arrow a few times to word 'exhilarating'. Cool idea if you ask me.

## Year planner 
When using normal calendars, it's hard to visualize overlapping episodes in my life. Let's say I know that for the next two weeks I will be working on my thesis. What I want to do is simply grab todays date in a large representation of entire year and drop it onto two weeks from now. The line could even say how much time i'm allocating. Afterwards, there would be a line in chosen color running between those two days, day by day. This way I could easily chart periods in my life. And if I wanted to add a new focus and would already see 4 lines running through today, I would know it's probably too much, without even knowing what those are. Of course I could check in a side panel where every color would be assigned a string with explanation and maybe dates shown. Cool idea if you ask me

## Timeline
Web app showing a single large timeline. Add the top there would be a button opening a form for entering a timeline event.
Event could have image attached, some text, date, location maybe.
Timeline would then show the event on the timeline at the appropriate location either in full length if short or only as a small preview which would get enlarged on hover.
Timeline would allow a person to visualize their life by their catalogued events. 
The main point of the timeline would be the visual side.
