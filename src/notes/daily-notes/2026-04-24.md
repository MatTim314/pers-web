# the void - in between
Imagine you're listening to music. The song is dowloaded on your plugged in computer. The headphones are wired and plugged in. Rent and electricity is paid. Stove is empty and all windows are closed. So there you are, in the void. Blissful space. Your attention actually, for once, belonging just to you. I yearn for the void. I believe we all do. My belief is that we were made to live in the void and all our adaptations are made in attempt to return to the void. We do not like not being in the void. It wears us down. Void is our solace, our divine resting grounds. In the void, we get to see a hair fall down through sunrays, we get to see our kids growing up not just grow up, the patterns steam makes as it leaves our hot tea.
